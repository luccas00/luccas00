## 👨🏼‍💻 About me

Senior Microsoft Dynamics 365 and Power Platform professional focused on designing, developing, integrating, and delivering enterprise business solutions.

Currently working as a **D365 CE Sr. Technical Consultant at Gambito**, collaborating with international delivery teams and enterprise clients.

My experience spans the full solution lifecycle, including requirements analysis, solution design, technical architecture, development, integrations, testing, deployment, ALM, CI/CD, and production support.

My core experience includes **Dynamics 365 CE, Customer Service, Sales, Project Operations, Dataverse, Power Apps, Power Automate, Power Pages, Copilot Studio, C#/.NET plugins, JavaScript, REST APIs, Azure, Azure DevOps, and Power Platform CLI**.

🎓 Pursuing a **B.Sc. in Information Systems** at the Federal University of Ouro Preto — UFOP.

🌎 Based in Brazil and working with international teams.
